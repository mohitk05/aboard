# Earth textures

- `earth-blue-marble.jpg` (day), `earth-night.jpg` (city lights), `earth-topology.png` (relief),
  `earth-water.png` (water mask): NASA Visible Earth / Earth Observatory imagery (Blue Marble,
  Black Marble), public domain.
- `night-sky.png`: starfield.

Copied from the [`three-globe`](https://github.com/vasturiano/three-globe) npm package's example
images (MIT License, © Vasco Asturiano).

## Streamed tiles

Zoomed-in imagery comes from NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), part of NASA EOSDIS: the `BlueMarble_NextGeneration` and `VIIRS_Black_Marble` layers. Public domain; we acknowledge NASA EOSDIS GIBS as the source.
